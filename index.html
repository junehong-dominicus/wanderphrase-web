WanderPhrase

Been there. Want to go. Say it local.

A community where travelers share the places they have actually been — stays, food, culture, sights — save the places they dream of going, and learn the local language of each place along the way.

Been There

Post the places you visited as trips of place cards: a photo, a short story, the one tip you wish you had known, and whether you would go again. Your cards become other travelers' trusted recommendations.

Want to Go

Save any place card to your wishlist map, grouped by city. When the trip finally happens, your saved cards become your draft itinerary — and afterwards, your own Been-There posts.

Say It Local

Every city has a phrase pack: greetings, directions, ordering food, checking in, emergencies, and etiquette. Learn with flashcards and quizzes, and earn a passport stamp for each city you complete.

Invite-only beta — coming soon

WanderPhrase launches first in Seoul and Vancouver, in Korean and English. The beta opens to invited testers first; public launch follows once each city is full of real travelers' cards.
