Privacy Policy — WanderPhrase

Effective date: 2026-11-16 · Draft for the invite-only beta

WanderPhrase ("the app") is developed by June Hong ("we", "us"), an independent developer in Vancouver, Canada. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: support@wanderphrase.com.

Data we collect

  • Account data. Your email address and sign-in provider (Google or email/password), managed by Firebase Authentication, plus the public profile you create in the app: handle, display name, bio, and profile photo.
  • Content you post. Trips and place cards (place names, stories, tips, ratings), photos you upload, likes, saves, follows, and reports. Photos are resized and stripped of EXIF metadata (including any GPS tags) before upload.
  • Place pins. A place card may include a pin you place on the map for a public venue. The app does not track your live location and does not collect location in the background.
  • Learning progress. Phrase-pack progress and city stamps linked to your account.
  • Technical data. Crash reports and basic device/app diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics, used only to fix problems.

How data is used

To operate the service: showing your trips and cards to other users according to the visibility you choose (public, followers, or private), running the wishlist and learning features, moderating reported content, and keeping the service secure. We do not sell your data. The beta contains no advertising and no third-party analytics beyond the crash reporting above.

Where data is stored

Account, content, and photo data are stored with Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) in the Montréal, Canada region (northamerica-northeast1). Google processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy and security terms.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your own trips and place cards at any time in the app.
  • You can delete your account and associated data in the app, or request deletion on the web: Delete your account.
  • Public content is visible to other signed-in users by design; choose the followers or private visibility for anything you do not want public.

Children

WanderPhrase is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are for users aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note the new effective date before the change applies to the beta.
