Terms of Use — WanderPhrase

Effective date: 2026-11-16 · Draft for the invite-only beta. This is the same text the app shows before your first post.

1. What WanderPhrase is

WanderPhrase lets you share places you have been, save places you want to go, and learn local phrases for each city. By creating an account or posting content, you accept these terms.

2. Your content

Post only your own photos and your own words. Do not post content that is illegal, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, violent, misleading, or that infringes someone else's rights. Photo location metadata (EXIF/GPS) is stripped before upload.

3. Zero tolerance for objectionable content

There is a zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content and abusive users. Content that violates these terms will be removed, and the accounts that post it will be banned. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours.

4. Reporting and blocking

Every card, trip, and profile can be reported in the app. You can block any user; blocked users' content is hidden from your feeds.

5. Wander Points

Wander Points are earned in the app only. They are never sold, have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and cannot be exchanged for money or goods outside the app.

6. Accounts

You are responsible for activity on your account. We may suspend or remove accounts that violate these terms. You may delete your account and your content at any time from the app or the account deletion page on our website.

7. Contact

Questions or safety concerns: support@wanderphrase.com
