Delete your WanderPhrase account

You can delete your account and your data at any time. There are two ways:

Option 1 — In the app

Open WanderPhrase, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, and confirm. Deletion starts immediately.

Option 2 — Web request

If you no longer have the app installed, send a deletion request from the email address you used to sign in:

Email a deletion request

We verify that the request comes from the account's email address and complete web-requested deletions within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (handle, display name, bio, photo) and sign-in account
  • Your trips and place cards, including uploaded photos
  • Your saves, likes, follows, learning progress, and stamps

Residual copies in routine backups are purged within 30 days of deletion. Content another user has saved remains in their private wishlist only as long as your card was public; once your account is deleted, your cards are removed from the service, including from other users' views.
